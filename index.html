About Us

Africa Workforce Training & Development, Inc. (AWTD)

AWTD is a formally incorporated, industry-led technical training initiative established to tackle unemployment and underemployment across Africa. We bridge the divide between academic instruction and real employer demand through short-cycle, workshop-first training verified by practical performance.

Training is designed backward from real jobs, real tools, and measurable wage and enterprise outcomes. Every cohort is built around strict safety culture, employer-assessed competency sign-off, and dual-track transition support, connecting graduates to skilled employment or backing those who cannot secure placement within three months to launch their own enterprise.

The Why

Africa's education system and its employers are not speaking. Nigeria's TVET enrolment sits below 10% of tertiary education, while employers report graduates who lack hands-on skills, safety culture, and work-readiness. The bridge between academic training and real employment is broken.

Nigeria Tertiary System Benchmark <10%

Share of tertiary enrolment in technical and vocational education, despite acute technician shortages across power, manufacturing, transport, and infrastructure.

The model

Industry-led, competency-based, and measured on outcomes. Every element is engineered to prevent training from becoming theoretical or school-like.

01 Standards

Employer-Led Demand & Standards

Sector councils define job roles and competency checklists. Employer Advisory Groups refresh demand signals quarterly and guide practical standards.

Continuous
02 Hubs

Competency-Based Training Hubs

Short, stackable modules: an eight-week certificate programme delivering 72 contact hours (three evenings per week, 18:00 to 21:00). 70% hands-on.

Workshop-first
03 Placement

Job Placement & Enterprise Backing

Graduates enter direct employer placement pipelines or receive AWTD entrepreneurship backing if not placed within three months. Paid apprenticeships are a long-term model component (not part of the Abuja pilot).

Dual-track pathway
04 Mobility

Stackable Credentials

Digital badges, performance portfolios, and certifications aligned to national qualifications. Learners earn a credential quickly and return to advance as needed.

Nationally recognised
05 Telemetry

QA, Data & Continuous Improvement

A live dashboard tracking completion, placement and retention. Weekly skills audits, monthly employer reviews, and anonymous learner feedback loops.

Transparent
06 Scale

The Replication Kit

The pilot doesn't only graduate technicians; it yields a scalable playbook of curriculum modules, assessment rubrics, instructor training materials, equipment lists, and employer MOU templates.

Scalable

Operating principles

01

Employment Outcomes First

Training is designed backward from real jobs, real tools, and measurable placement and wage gains. If it doesn't lead to work, it doesn't belong in the curriculum.

02

Industry-Aligned Excellence

Employers co-design competencies, assessments, and credentials. Quarterly refresh cycles keep standards current with market demand.

03

Access and Equity

Affordable, inclusive pathways that expand opportunity for youth, women, and underserved communities, with targeted supports to reach those excluded from traditional education routes.

04

Hands-On Mastery

Competency-based learning grounded in practice, safety, and demonstrated performance instead of seat time. 70% of every programme is workshop-based.

05

Stackable Pathways

Clear, modular credentials that let learners earn quickly and return to advance over time. Career mobility is built into the architecture from the start.

06

Accountability & Improvement

Data-driven decisions, transparent standards, and rapid iteration based on results. We publish what we measure, including what isn't working.
