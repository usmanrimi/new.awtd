AWTD Entrepreneurship Track

Separate from the technical trade tracks, the board has agreed an AWTD entrepreneurship track running alongside Electrical and Welding. Graduates who cannot find employment within three months may be backed to start their own business.

Dual-Track Outcome Guarantee

Technical competence is only half the mission. AWTD ensures no verified graduate is left without a viable economic pathway.

01 · Parallel Track

Alongside Electrical & Welding

Entrepreneurship training runs alongside the Abuja technical certificate tracks, equipping trainees with costing, quoting, customer service, and basic financial discipline.

02 · Three-Month Backstop

Enterprise Launch Backing

Graduates who clear Capstone Validation and cannot secure wage employment within three months may be backed by AWTD to launch an independent trade business.

03 · Separate Application

Dedicated Intake Route

Prospective candidates applying specifically for the AWTD entrepreneurship training track submit their interest separately from standard trade-track intake.
