Why WTDA Exists

Africa's education system
and its employers
are not speaking.

Nigeria's TVET enrolment sits below 10% of tertiary education — while employers report graduates who lack hands-on skills, safety culture, and work-readiness. The bridge between academic training and real employment is broken.

The Status Quo

  • Years-long, school-like and theoretical programmes
  • Isolated from industry — no employer co-design
  • Assessment by written exam only
  • Static curricula that don't reflect market demand
  • TVETs perceived as second-tier and under-resourced
  • Long time-to-productivity after hiring

The WTDA Blueprint

  • Months-long, short-cycle & stackable modules
  • Employers co-design competencies from day one
  • Performance-based portfolios & certifications
  • Built for rapid iteration and replication
  • Workshop-first: hands-on before theory
  • Paid apprenticeships with employer mentors

The WTDA Model

Industry-led.
Competency-based.
Outcomes measured.

A public-private model that co-designs training with employers and verifies skills through practical assessments. Every element is engineered to prevent the programme from becoming theoretical or school-like.

01 🏭

Employer-Led Demand & Standards

Sector councils define job roles and competency checklists. Employer Advisory Groups (EAGs) refresh demand signals quarterly and provide mentors for work-based learning.

Continuous
02 🔧

Competency-Based Training Hubs

Short, stackable modules: Foundation Core (2–4 weeks) followed by trade-specific Technical Tracks (8–16 weeks). 70% hands-on. Strict curriculum safeguards prevent drift toward theory.

Workshop-first
03 📋

Paid Apprenticeships & Placement

Structured work-based learning at partner employer sites, with clear outcomes and conversion targets from apprenticeship to employment. Every trainee completes a Capstone Validation project.

Required pathway
04 🎓

Stackable Credentials

Digital badges, performance portfolios, and certifications aligned to national qualifications. Learners earn a credential quickly and return to advance their career as needed.

Nationally recognised
05 📊

QA, Data & Continuous Improvement

Live dashboard tracking completion, placement, and retention. Weekly skills audits, monthly employer reviews, and anonymous learner feedback loops. Data-driven decisions at every level.

Transparent
06 📦

The Replication Kit

The pilot doesn't just graduate 60 technicians — it yields a scalable playbook: curriculum modules, assessment rubrics, instructor training materials, MVL equipment lists, and employer MOU templates.

Scalable

How We Measure Success

Outcomes first.
Every metric earned.

Training is designed backward from real jobs, real tools, and measurable placement and wage gains. These are the KPIs tracked quarterly across every cohort.

Primary Target
ZERO
Safety Incidents

Safety is a gate, not a guideline. Repeated safety violations pause learner progression. Target is strictly near zero.

Placement
70%+
Jobs in 90 Days

Placement rate measured within 60–90 days of graduation. Nigeria pilot target: at least 70% placed in jobs or apprenticeships.

Competency
100%
Practical Sign-off

Every graduate must pass employer-assessed competency verification before receiving credentials. No written-exam shortcut.

Equity
↑
Female Participation

Targeted recruitment and non-traditional trade supports to actively increase women's participation across all tracks.

Tracked Quarterly
Completion Rate

Enrollment-to-completion attrition measured and reported per cohort.

Leading Indicator
Attendance & Punctuality

Day-one readiness signal. Tracked from intake audition week onward.

Employer Feedback
Satisfaction Score

Direct scoring from the Employer Advisory Group. Target: above 4.0/5.0.

What We Stand For

Six principles that
cannot be negotiated.

Employment Outcomes First

Training is designed backward from real jobs, real tools, and measurable placement and wage gains. If it doesn't lead to work, it doesn't belong in the curriculum.

Industry-Aligned Excellence

Employers co-design competencies, assessments, and credentials to ensure relevance and quality. Quarterly refresh cycles keep standards current with market demand.

Access and Equity

Affordable, inclusive pathways that expand opportunity for youth, women, and underserved communities. Targeted supports to reach those excluded from traditional education routes.

Hands-On Mastery

Competency-based learning grounded in practice, safety, and demonstrated performance — not seat time. 70% of every programme is workshop-based.

Stackable Pathways

Clear, modular credentials that enable learners to earn quickly and return to advance over time. Career mobility built into the architecture from the start.

Accountability & Improvement

Data-driven decisions, transparent standards, and rapid iteration based on results. We publish what we measure — including what isn't working.

The 6-Month Roadmap
Weeks 1–6 / Month 1–2

Phase 1: Discovery & Design

Validate local demand, choose tracks, secure site/equipment, train instructors, finalize assessments.

Weeks 6–20 / Month 2–5

Phase 2: Cohort 1

Foundation Core, Track Training, and Capstone. Work-based learning placements begin week 6.

Month 3–5 (concurrent)

Phase 3: Cohort 2 & Refine

Apply lessons from Cohort 1, tighten standards, stabilize costs, prepare replication documentation.

Month 6

Decision Gate: Scale

If targets are achieved across two cohorts, authorize packaging of the model for replication to additional countries and sites.
