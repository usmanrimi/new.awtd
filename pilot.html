The Nigeria pilot

The Abuja proof of concept is free to attend (no stipend and no transport provided) and produces the Replication Kit for Kaduna and subsequent rollout sites.

Pilot specifications & execution roadmap

Pilot LocationAbuja
Pilot StartFirst week of March 2027
Cohort Size20 trainees (1 cohort)
Pilot TracksElectrical & Welding
Certificate8-week certificate
Schedule3 evenings/wk · 18:00–21:00
Contact Hours72 contact hours
Fees & SupportFree · No stipend/transport
Pre-Launch · Through Feb 2027

Phase 1: Discovery & Readiness

Validate local demand in Abuja, ready Electrical and Welding bays, onboard instructors, and finalise practical assessments.

Weeks 1–4 · March 2027

Phase 2: Core & Workshop Foundations

Cohort of 20 trainees begins the first week of March 2027. Safety culture, tools mastery, and evening workshop sessions (18:00–21:00).

Weeks 5–8 · April 2027

Phase 3: Capstone & Transition

Complete 72 contact hours, clear employer-assessed Capstone Validation, and enter job placement or 3-month entrepreneurship backing.

Post-Pilot · Governance Gate

Decision Gate: Scale to Kaduna & Beyond

Evaluate cohort placement and enterprise outcomes, refine the Replication Kit, and authorise expansion to Kaduna and additional sites.

The Replication Kit deliverables

Modular Curriculum & Rubrics

Validated Foundation Core and trade-specific Technical Track modules paired with employer-scored practical assessment rubrics.

Standardised

Instructor & MVL Equipment Lists

Master trainer onboarding materials, safety gate protocols, and Minimum Viable Lab (MVL) workshop equipment specifications.

Turnkey setup

Employer MOU Templates

Field-tested Employer Advisory Group charters, work-based learning host agreements, and Capstone co-assessment protocols.

Ready to scale
Hub-and-Spoke Delivery

Pilot Hubs (Abuja + Kaduna)

Central technical hubs coordinate curriculum quality and Capstone assessment while spoke facilities and employer partner sites host practical instruction and work-based learning.
